GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered
plainly.

It’s sensible to understand the process before sharing information or making a decision.

Can you remove any debt from my credit report?

No one should promise to remove accurate, valid information simply because a consumer requests it. If information is inaccurate, outdated or otherwise eligible for correction or removal, the appropriate process and evidence are needed. A credit-report correction does not automatically cancel the underlying debt. The National Credit Regulator FAQ explains that removing adverse information does not mean you are no longer liable for the debt.

Can you remove a debt-review flag?

Debt-review information is handled through specific procedures. For example, section 71 of the National Credit Act provides for a debt counsellor to issue a clearance certificate when statutory requirements are met. A credit bureau or national register then expunges the debt-review information after receiving the certificate. Read section 71 of the National Credit Act.

Can you guarantee a result?

No. Each matter depends on its facts, supporting documents, legal requirements and, where relevant, decisions by creditors, courts, debt counsellors or credit bureaus.

Do I need to share documents?

We may need documents relevant to your matter, such as a credit report, account statements or correspondence. We’ll tell you what is needed and why. Never send passwords, PINs or one-time banking codes.
